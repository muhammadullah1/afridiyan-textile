About Afridiyan

Afridiyan Imperial Textile Mall (Private) Limited is a well-known and trusted cloth shop located in the heart of Bara Bazar, Khyber.

With years of experience in the textile business, we proudly serve our customers with quality products and reliable service. Our mission is to deliver the best fabrics at the best prices while maintaining the highest levels of customer satisfaction.

Whether you're looking for the finest gents suiting cloth, beautiful ladies fabric, or the latest seasonal collections — we have it all under one roof. Come experience honest, friendly service and a wide range of premium textiles at Afridiyan Imperial.
